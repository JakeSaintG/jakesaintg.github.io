# May as well learn to make SVGs

July 5 2025

As I moved on to the React base dex, I spent a little bit of time trying to make a decent Pokédex header with CSS. I got pretty close but I wasn’t producing something that really stood out as a good “final product”. I probably could have gotten there with a little more time, and a few more border radii, but I decided that this may be a good use case for a custom SVG. I had Inkscape installed and had messed around in it a little for a few other projects but this felt like as good of a time as any to hone a different skill. 

So I cracked open inkscape and got to crafting! After, admittedly, much more time than it would have taken me with CSS, I produced a PokéDex header that I could be proud of!

The issue is that it won’t scale to different screen sizes very well, even if I separated the lights, but I think a good solution to that would be to split it out into 3 chunks. The left most would be from the end to passed the middle after the curve. The right most would be basically just the tip of the right end. Everything in between would connect the two and would stretch with different screen sizes. This would allow it to stretch/scale without causing the border on the curve from potentially distorting. Work in progress!

After I put a little bit more time into messing around in Inkscape, I decided to make a custom grass icon as well. FontAwesome got me most of the way there but it didn’t have something that looked like the tall grass from the games. So, I took the setting gear icon, duplicated it for the icon size, and over wrote it with a homemade grass icon. I’ll keep tweaking it over time but I’m also pretty proud of how it turned out.
